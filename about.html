Our Story & Concept

Satti Cafe is a modern restaurant offering a fusion of Eastern, national Kazakh, Japanese, and European cuisine. Located in the heart of Astana at Mangilik El 16/1, we provide a unique atmosphere for family dinners, friendly meetings, and business lunches.

Every detail of our interior has been carefully crafted to build a warm, authentic, and welcoming experience for every guest.

BOZA Brewery

One of the distinctive features of Satti Cafe is our own internal BOZA brewery. We brew fresh, high-quality craft drinks directly on-site and serve them straight from the tanks to your table at the main bar.

Cozy Summer Terrace

During the warm season, our guests can relax on our spacious summer terrace. Surrounded by green plants and comfortable seating under our iconic white tent, it is the perfect place to enjoy warm Astana evenings.
