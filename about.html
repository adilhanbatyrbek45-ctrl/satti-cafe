Our Story

Satti Cafe is a modern restaurant with Eastern, national, Japanese and European cuisine. The cafe also has its own BOZA brewery, where fresh beer is made. The beer is served directly from the tanks at the bar.

The restaurant has a special design, and every detail of the interior has its own meaning.
